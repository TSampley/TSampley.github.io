title: Everything Happens for a Reason
date: 2025-10-11 2000 -0600
---

"Everything happens for a reason." It's a phrase frequently heard by anyone having a hard time, especially when consoled by people who keep themselves blissfully ignorant by ignoring the suffering in the world they could do something about.

I can't deny that everything happens for a reason, in the causal sense[^1] – everything has some prior cause leading to the effect of the happening, but I can't humor the idea that everything in this cruel world happens for a reason[^2], in the sense of purpose. Assuming that everything in this world happens for some kind of "good reason[^2]" also means accepting that all the bad things that happen are equally justified by their contribution to whatever the grand purpose or objective is.
 
 [^1]: Reason (causal) - The action or state which led to some response or change in state.
 [^2]: Reason (purpose) - Some motivating objective for which some action is taken.
