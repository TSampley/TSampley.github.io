Once a species begins to dominate an ecosystem, it no longer competes primarily with its environment or competing species – the primary [competitor](/science/biology/ecology/competition) becomes members of its own species.

A long, long time ago, when humanity began to grow rapidly beyond our extended family units, and the weight of reputation began to wane with our ability to retain connections with an increasing number of neighbors, the cost of antisocial behaviors began to decrease while the benefits increase with a greater number of prosocial individuals.

Cane toads in Australia now exhibit cannabalism, because when a species dominates an ecosystem so thoroughly, the easiest way to get the resources you need to grow is to simply eat members of your species.
