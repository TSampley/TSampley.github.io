The meaning of your life is the sum of all direct impacts you've had on the world and in-turn their influences [^1].

I desperately want my life to have meaning, but by the simple definition, everyone's life has meaning, because we all have some effect on the world, no matter how small or common, i.e. replaceable, a life might seem. Even the tiniest of bacteria has meaning in that sense – it's meaning is usually microscopic, but in aggregate, they're macroscopically meaningful during outbreaks, which emphasizes my struggle: I don't just want to have any kind of meaningful life. A selfish, greedy elitist who signs away the homes or lives of thousands of people clearly has a meaningful life in the same way as a bacterial outbreak. I want to not just have *some* impact on the world, I want that to be a positive impact - the greatest possible positive impact. Whenever I see someone in need, I feel compelled to help them – it's just the way I was raised – but I'm constantly worried that the effort and resources I give others will be wasted, like a neutron being absorbed by lead instead of releasing more neutrons.

The frustrating part is that the liberals I typically find myself around are awfully naive and performative: they're very happy to blatently waste their resources because it makes them feel like they did something. They lack the critical self-awareness to really think about what they're doing – it just feels right, and that's all they know. On top of that, there's just as much problematic dogma in liberal rhetoric as there is in conservative rhetoric – it's just painted with rainbows, so they don't recognize its toxicity.

I'm stuck between uneducated, discompassionate conservatives, and unrealistic, naive liberals, but I'm no moderate – I'm just left of most "left"-ists and a progressive, not a simple liberal. There is too much lead in this world. The rich, fissile material I'm looking for out there in the world seems too scarce. What I need is a human gas-centrifuge, some way to quickly filter out the good from the waste so I can finally build this bomb of progress and wipe out this digusting ignorance.
