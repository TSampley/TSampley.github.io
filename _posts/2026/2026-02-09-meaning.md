The meaning of your life is the sum of all direct impacts you've had on the world and in-turn their influences [^1].

I desperately want my life to have meaning, but by the simple definition, everyone's life has meaning, because we all have some effect on the world, no matter how small or common, i.e. replaceable, a life might seem. Even the tiniest of bacteria has meaning in that sense – it's meaning is usually microscopic, but in aggregate, they're clearly very impactful during outbreaks. Which emphasizes my struggle – I don't just want to have any kind of meaningful life – a selfish, greedy elitist who signs away the homes or lives of thousands of people clearly has a meaningful life in the same way as a bacterial outbreak.
