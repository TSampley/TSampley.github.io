I subscribe to the belief that language is just a social subform of broader communication, which originates from the ability of social creatures to empathize and/or understand that another member is feeling what they would be feeling if they were making the same expression. Body language is used throughout the animal kingdom. Dogs sneeze in a way that is understood to be a laugh, and rats giggle when tickled - both ways of communicating to their peers that an otherwise threatening behavior is entirely harmless or enjoyable.
