Art, in its most general understanding, can be any composition of space and 
materials with some purpose or value. Anything designated art is art. A
vast natural landscape becomes art as soon as someone walking along a path
views the expanse and, suddenly struck by the beauty, decides "that's art!",
just as much as a nondescript lump of material whose creator has poured 
hundreds of hours into their masterpiece, just as much as a child's card
on the refrigerator.
