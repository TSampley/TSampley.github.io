Since this is a sensitive subject, I want to be perfectly clear that I am not advocating suicide. I am personally annoyed at how frequently people indulge appeal to nature fallacies, so I do understand the need to clearly differentiate – just because I describe an evolutionary process, a natural process, does not mean that I think that process is good or advocate it in any way.

Rape is a behavioral sexual adaptation that – quite simply – works, at least in environments where there aren't legal restrictions against it. The efficacy of some behavior in the natural world should not be taken as a justification or reason to emulate that natural behavior. Herbert Spencer's use of Darwinism to justify open markets and lawless "free-er" societies was insane, and the continued use of derived justifications since then continues to be just as insane. When we observe a behavior within society and someone thinks to emulate that behavior simply because it was observed, we call that bandwagon fallacy. Spencerist arguments in favor of policies that emulate nature are a sort of appeal to nature fallacy that broadens the sampling of a bandwagon fallacy to other species. Rape occurs frequently in other species, and only an insane person would think that means we should allow rape to happen in civil society.
