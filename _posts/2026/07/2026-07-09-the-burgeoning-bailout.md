- possibility of coming AI bailout
- AI leaders leaning on security threat from Chinese competition
- elites' interest in AI's success
- Altman stated that OpenAI would not take a bailout - that leadership would allow it to fail

- Trump Admin claims there won't be a bailout [^cadtm-openai].
- similar claims were made by auto manufacturers and banks before their big bailouts
- leaked email from OpenAI urges White House to consider different fiscal models to provide funds to OpenAI to compete with China
- Elizabeth Warren has pointed out the same inconsistencies and incentives[^warren-openai].
