Languages are primarily identified by their ISO-639-1 code[^1].



```

- name: Chinese
  id: ch
- name: English
  id: en
- id: fr
  name: French
- id: he
  name: Hebrew
- name: Japanese
  id: jp
- name: Spanish
  id: es
  
```


[^1]: [ISO-639-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/74575.html)
