## Donuts
- 1 C milk
- 2 1/4 t yeast
- 1 T sugar
- 1/4 C sugar
- 2 eggs, large
- 6 T butter
- 1 t vanilla
- 1 t nutmeg
- 1 t cinnamon
- 1/2 t salt
- 2 C all-purpose flour
- 2 C all-purpose flour
- 1-2 Q frying oil

## Vanilla Glaze
- 2 C powdered sugar
- 1/3 C milk
- 1/2 t vanilla

## Chocolate Icing
- 1 1/2 oz baker's semi-sweet chocolate
- 2 T unsalted butter
- 2 t honey
- 2 t water

- whisk together milk, yeast, sugar
- add remaining sugar, eggs, butter, vanilla, spices, and flour, and mix on low for 1 min
- add remaining flour 1 T at a time until dough pulls away from sides of mixer (up to an additional 1/2 C of flour as necessary).
- place in oiled bowl and rest 1.5-2 hours
- punch down and roll out onto floured surface to 1/2" thickness
- cut and shape into donuts
- let rise covered for 30 minutes
- fry in 375º oil for 1-2 min each side
