## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 C flour
- 1 1/4 t baking powder
- 1/2 t baking soda
- 2/3 C sugar
- 1/3 C shortening
- 2 eggs
- 2 T milk
- 1 C mashed ripe banana
- 1/4 chopped nuts

## Directions

- Combine flour, baking powder, soda, and 3/4 t salt
- beat sugar and shortening
- add eggs, one at a time, and milk, beting till smooth after each addition
- fold in nuts
- turn batter into greased 8x4x2 in loaf pan
- bake in 350º oven for 60-65 min
