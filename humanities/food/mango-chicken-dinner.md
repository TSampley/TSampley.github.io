## Ingredients

- 2 chicken breast
- 1 mango
- 1 T onion powder
- 1/2 T garlic powder
- 1 T sugar
- 1/4 t salt
- 1 t chipotle powder
- 1 T vinegar
- 1 T lemon juice
- 1 C head country barbecue sauce

## Directions

- place chicken breasts in metal pan with sprinkle of salt to season
- dice mango and place in small bowl
- add onion powder, garlic powder, sugar, salt, chipotle powder, vinegar, lemon juice, and mix
- combine marinade with chicken breasts, coating all sides of the chicken
- allow to marinade for 4-12 hours
- place chicken and mango in slow cooker with barbecue sauce
- cook on 300º for 4 hours
