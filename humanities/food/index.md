Anything that can be safely consumed can be treated as food, but some nutritive value is usually assumed.

Humans can accept an especially wide variety of foods, even for omnivores, which has allowed our diets to vary as materials are available in our environments. This regional variety has shaped the local cuisines of cultures around the world, leading to distinct dishes that are the pride of the people they represent.
