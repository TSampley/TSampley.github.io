## Ingredients

- 2 C warm water
- 1 C whole wheat flour
- 1 T yeast
- 2 T honey
- 2 T mollasses
- 1/4 C vegetable oil
- 1 1/2 t salt
- 5 C flour, all-purpose

## Instructions

- mix warm water, whole wheat flour, and sugars, then add yeast and allow to activate for 5-8 minutes
- soak for 30 minutes, stirring periodically
- add vegetable oil, salt, and 2 C of remaining flour
- work in remainder of flour until integrated
- cover and allow to rest for 1 hour
- punch down, divide into two loaves
- shape and cover loaves, and allow to rise 30 minutes to 1 hour
- bake in oven at 350ºF for around 30 minutes, depending on humidity and elevation
