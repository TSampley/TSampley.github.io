## Ingredients

- 2 C flour
- 3 t baking powder
- 2 t sugar
- 1/2 t cream of tartar
- 1/4 t salt
- 1/2 C shortening
- 2/3 C milk

## Directions

- combine flour, baking powder, sugar, cream of tartar, and salt
- cut in shortening till mixture resembles small peas or crumbs
- add milk to well in the center
- stir just till dough clings together
- fold and flatten on a floured surface about 10 times
- shape into biscuits
- bake at 450º for about 10 minutes
