## Abrahamic Faiths
The Abrahamic faiths all derive from mythos around the lineage of a paternal figure, Abraham.

### Judaism
Oldest of Abrahamic faiths. Texts: Torah, Talmud (spoken tradition now written)

Like many stereotypes, Jewish stereotypes are broad overgeneralizations that have a small fragment of basis in reality. In particular, the variety of finance stereotypes ultimately stem from financial prescriptions in the Talmud which, among other things, instructs followers to separate their earnings into thirds. If a religion, as a vehicle of cultural cohesion, prescribes specific financial policies which happen to be good advice, then even if your followers don't understand the reasoning behind it, they'll still do well simply by adhering to the religion; it should therefore not be surprising that a good number of Jews are well-off – no conspiracy necessary, just sound financial advice given through a shared religion.
