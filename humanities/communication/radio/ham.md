Amateur radio, a.k.a. "ham radio", is the practice of operating radio transmitters and receivers for hobby or emergency purposes.

## Licensing

To legally operate outside 

### Technician

- 35 questions on radio theory, regulations, operating practices
- access to all amateur bands above 30MHz

### General

- 35 question exam
- some privileges on all amateur bands and modes

### Amateur Extra

- 50 questions
- all privileges on all bands and modes
