Burpee has been accused of using GMO seeds, but the company denies the accusations, and there has been no evidence for the accusations. They have also been criticized for their size; for anyone interested in supporting smaller, local businesses, libraries and farmers markets usually have information about where to get seeds locally if they don't have some themselves.
