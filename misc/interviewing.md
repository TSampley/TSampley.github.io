Interviews are meant to be assessments of a candidate's fitness for a role in an organization.

## Social
A social interview, often called a culture-fit interview, is an assessment of a candidates social behaviors 

### Trap
A trap is a leading question that is meant to suggest an answer counter to the company's culture. It's meant to test a candidate for pushback to see if they prioritize their values over conformity. This is not a prevalent type of question because most companies are looking for conformance.

## Technical
Technical interviews take many forms depending on the work environment and prefernces of the hiring team.

### Leet Code
Probably the easiest, and least interactive, but also the cheapest for most companies. There are many sites that offer leetcode challenges and the ability for companies to put together their own questions and sets. Because of the lack of interactivity, it is also usually the easiest to cheat, while also being the worst evaluation of a candidate.

[leetcode.com](https://leetcode.com/)
[hackerrank.com](https://www.hackerrank.com/)

### Live Coding
Slightly more interactive

### Design
Design exercises are by nature more in-depth than other technical interviews

#### Whiteboarding
A whiteboard interview is a design interview using a physical or digital whiteboard, or drafting surface, in order to diagram and work out a problem more rigorously.
