title: Terrorism
description: The use of terror as a political tool.

date: 2026-09-24 0001 -0500
---

Fear is a reasonable response to many situations, and the idea that we should fear some things is also perfectly valid from a rhetorical perspective; however, when one goes beyond simple fear and actively seeks to commit some act or show force in a way that makes a target population fear death or other imminent threat to the point of inciting terror, that becomes terrorism. 

That doesn't necessarily mean that it doesn't have rhetorical or persuasive value, as the US military will tell you itself[^wiki-us-military], even though they prefer to refer to it as "Shock and Awe" instead of "terrorism". Meanwhile, they vilify Al-Qaeda, and many other activist groups, for their use of terrorism, which is often the only tool available to them, thanks to the exploitation, manipulation, and oppression of the Middle East at large by the CIA and military just for the sake of destablizing Russia.

When faced with an oppressive regime that refuses to treat you as human beings, terrorism is a perfectly valid strategy.
