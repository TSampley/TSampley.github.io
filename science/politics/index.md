Politics concerns any human activity related to organizing for the sake of regulating human activity. The word derives from latin "polis" meaning "of the people"<sup>[citation needed]</sup>, so any discussion or activity relating to the people is of a political nature. Even something as apparently apolitical as attending a football game is technically a political action. The word today has unfortunately become conflated with [contentious](../sociology/contention). Usually, when someone says that they're "apollitical" they're actually trying to say they avoid contentious discussions.
