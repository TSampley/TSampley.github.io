A dictatorship is a form of authoritarian government where all power resides in the hands of the single dictator.

| Position | Portion | Power | Individual Power Factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citizens | 95% | 0% | 0 |
| Regime | ~5% | 90% | 18 |
| Dictator | .01% | 10% | 1000 |
