As a political philosophy, Communism has been conflated with [Leninism](./leninism), [Stalinism](./stalinism), and [Maoism](./maoism). The term "Communism" was coined by [Karl Marx](/humanities/history/europe/germany/karl-marx) for his analysis of societies' economies across history, producing also [Conflict Theory](/science/sociology/conflict-theory) and [Critical Theory](/science/sociology/critical-theory)

Often conflated with [socialism](./socialism).
