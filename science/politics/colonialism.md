Colonialism is an extractive economic arrangement where some external power exerts influence over a region or people in order to derive some economic benefit. The difference is the same as between [hard power](./power#hard-power) and [soft power](./power#soft-power).

## Old Colonialism
The violence of old colonialism was overt and the presence of the colonizer was undeniable.
