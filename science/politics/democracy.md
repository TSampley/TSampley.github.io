Democracy, as a [liberal](./liberalism) ideology, has only a few foundational principles; they can be difficult to uphold at times, because they require more humility than we are regularly called on to practice – in fact, in so-called "American" culture, we're even encouraged to act with excessive pride. Narcissistic behavior seems to be on the rise globally, but it was certainly pioneered in the U.S.

Principles of Democracy:
- Any government must rule only with the consent of the governed.
- Any government is composed of members of the governed. There are no separate classes of rulers and subjects.
