Biology (approx. the study of life[^etym]) is an incredibly broad field dealing with all forms of life, from the smallest bacterium or viral particle, to the largest aggregate lifeforms[^ncbi].

Understanding biology depends heavily on [chemistry](../chemistry/).

## Cytology
study of cells, their structure, and function

## Microbiology
study of microorganisms, or organisms on the microscopic scale, usually much smaller than 1mm.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacillus_anthracis
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eukaryotic_large_ribosomal_subunit_(60S)

## Physiology
biological mechanisms and interactions, focused on physical dynamics.
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK518698/
- https://www.britannica.com/science/physiology

## Biochemistry
study of chemistry specific to life, such as lipids, proteins, sugars, carbohydrates, and other organic molecules.
- prions exist in intermediate between life and nonlife
  - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1303448/

## Pathology
study of diseases.
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK518792/
- https://www.britannica.com/science/pathology
- prions: https://vet.ucalgary.ca/news/chronic-wasting-disease-may-transmit-humans-research-finds

### Therapy
the application of techniques and/or compounds to treat ailments

### Phytotherapy
the application of plant-derived compounds to treat ailments. naturally depends heavily on botany (phytology).

## Botany
study of plant biology
- California Poppy Uses: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10017456/
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK518834/

## Important Terms

### Death
Death is the point at which an organism can no longer maintain homeostasis 

### Dying
Dying is the process of nearing death. The term is usually reserved for some final, acute decline in health, but can also be used more generally, such as when people say "we're all dying".

### Killing
Killing is the process of bringing an organism to the state of [death](#death).
