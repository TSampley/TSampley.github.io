Biology is an incredibly broad field dealing with all forms of life, from the smallest bacterium or viral particle, to the largest aggregate lifeforms.

## Death
Death is the point at which an organism can no longer maintain homeostasis 

## Dying
Dying is the process of nearing death. The term is usually reserved for some final, acute decline in health, but can also be used more generally, such as when people say "we're all dying".

## Killing
Killing is the process of bringing an organism to the state of [death](#death).
