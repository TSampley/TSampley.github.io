### Online
Algorithms that operate on sequences are considered online if they can operate progressively, one symbol at a time, in contrast to needing the entire sequence before producing a result.

#### Ukonen's Suffix Tree
Ukonen's suffix tree algorithm is a way of constructing a suffix tree in O(n) time, a big improvement over the O(n^3) of a naive implementation.
