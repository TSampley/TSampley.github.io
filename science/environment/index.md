Environmental science is a broad, interdisciplinary study the encompasses chemistry, biology, geology, and meteorology.

Recycling is the process of breaking down an object to reuse the materials it is made of. Many biological processes qualify as recycling, but we're usually concerned with the human logistical system of recycling when we talk about it.
