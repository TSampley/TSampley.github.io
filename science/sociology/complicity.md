Complicity is not binary – it exists in degrees. While the killing of the United Healthcare CEO, Brian Thompson, is obviously just, he is not the sole individual responsoble for the unjust deaths of hundreds of thousands of underserved, innocent human beings. A CEO is often portrayed as the king of a company, but all executive positions are controlled by the board in a typical corporate business structure. The board is just as – if not more – responsible for the lost lives and unnecessary suffering caused by the policies of United Healthcare.
