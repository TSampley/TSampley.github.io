A conspiracy is simply a type of [cooperation](/science/biology/ecology/cooperation) where a group acts for their own benefit, while undermining another group or the general population.

## Conspiracy Theories
The term "conspiracy theory" has been sensationalized to the point that it is most often interpreted to mean "crackpot theories", including cryptid cover-ups, and other inane, persistent hallucinations. It *should* refer to a hypothetical conspiracy that someone is suggesting may exist. Conspiracies in fact have happened, and since the same elements of power and secrecy exist that motivated and allowed conpsiracies in the past, we should expect there to be conspiracies actively operating today and in the future.
