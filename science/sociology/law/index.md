Law is best studied as a social science or socio-economic engineering effort. It is heavily influenced by the humanities and [philosophy](../../philosophy/) in particular, but the practice itself is at times painfully formal, which gives it an air of authority, yet it can frequently be notoriously subjective – e.g. "I know it when I see it".
