Feelings are much simpler than thought by most humans; they are a biological experience of an individual's own internal physicochemical state in response to external stimuli or internal stimuli (perception).

Compare with [emotions](./emotions), the outward expression of presented feelings.

## Popularly Known Feelings



Anger
Sadness
Surprise
Joy
Love
Fear
Happy
Excited
Anxious
Tired
Energetic
