The central nervous system takes in information from the sensory/peripheral nervous system, processes it, and stimulates a corresponding signal in the motor/peripheral nervous system.

## Brain

The brain is the most significant portion of the entire [central nervous system](./cns).

### Cerebrum
Contains cortical columns

#### Corpus Collosum
Connects the two sides of the cerebrum. Contains the pineal gland.

#### Lobes
The cerebrum is typically divided into four lobes: the frontal, parietal, occipital, and temporal lobes.

##### Occipital
main processing center for sight.

##### Temporal
main processing center for sound; also responsible for sense of time.

Contains the amygdala.

##### Frontal
responsible for planning and higher-abstract processing.

##### Parietal
Touch, proprioception[^parietal] [^parietal2].
