#### Taste, Gustatory
Taste is a fairly basic, though critical, chemosensory system. It provides a basic sense of caloric and nutritive content through sweet, salt, and umami, or potential toxins through sour and bitter.

##### Sweet
Typically stimulated through the hydroxymethyl functional groups of sugars[^hydroxymethyl]. When our sense of sweetness is stimulated, it kicks off a series of biological reactions throughout our digestive system to get ready to break down, absorb, and store the sugar our body believes its detected. 

##### Salty
Stimulated by ionic compounds, usually common table salt, sodium chloride.

##### Sour
Stimulated by low pH (acidic) compounds.

##### Bitter
Stimulated by high pH (alkaline) compounds

##### Umami, Savory
Typically stimulated by proteins or amino acids.

#### Smell, Olfactory
Smell is also a chemosensory system like taste, but it is much more complex.

#### Sight

##### Focal Vision
The highest density of optical cells are found near the center of the retina on the back of the eye. This area is known as your focal vision.

##### Peripheral Vision
A lower density of optical cells is found further from the center of the retina, allowing less detailed vision over 90º to either side of center[^vision-peripheral].

##### Color Vision
This division of vision is sensed by optical cells called cones, which are further subdivided into red, green, and blue sensitive cells. Color vision works best in light conditions and is most concentrated in the focal vision range.

##### Black-and-White Vision
This division of vision is sensed by optical cells called rods, which are found in higher concetrations in peripheral vision and are responsible for sensing motion and more sensitive than cones in low light conditions.

##### Shape
Our vision system provides us with some level of geometric, or shape, information; however, this recognition appears to be fairly heavily influenced by early exposure to different shapes within the environment, particularly when the proprioceptive systems are stimulated simulateneously, providing deep, spatial, geometric information.
