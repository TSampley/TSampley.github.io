- study of cells, their structure, and function

- subfield of biology

- study of diseases in living organisms

pharmacology:
- study of chemical compounds effects on biology
- subfield of chemistry
- depends on biology

toxicology:
- study of noxious or toxic subtances and their effects on biology
- subfield of pharmacology
- depends on pathology

therapy:
- application of techniques for curative effects
- depends on toxicology, pharmacology
- https://www.etymonline.com/word/therapy

phytotherapy:
- the application of plant-derived compounds to treat ailments
- subfield of therapy
- depends on botany (phytology)
