"Science" is a very big topic that encompasses many fields, including natural sciences, social sciences, formal sciences, and applied sciences. This domain branch includes the natural, social sciences, and applied sciences. The formal sciences (a.k.a. math) are in the [Math branch](../math/). Science, as the collection of fields developed using the scientific method, is a nearly all-encompassing topic because it was born out of philosophy, specifically [epistemology](/philosophy/epistemology/) and realism, which attempt to explain the nature of existence and all its systems within.

The purpose of any science is to understand the world through the lens of that particular domain. Though we may be driven intrinsically by curiosity, we are ultimately interested in scientific knowledge because it grants us control over our environment through understanding of its fundamental workings; although, despite the ultimate purpose of science being its usefulness, the potential applications for some new knowledge or technology aren't always obvious immediately after discovery. Deep curiosity and a thirst for knowledge should be fed as much, and whenever, possible.

It's natural for businesses – who have limited budgets and a need to drive revenue to at least pay their staff – to worry about investing resources into something with the greatest chance of a return. Meanwhile, governments are ideally investing in their people, usually in infrastructure and utilities; however, scientific research has broad applications across indsutries to the benefit of the people and broader society – much like infrastructure and utilies – and is easily a worthy investment of tax revenue.
