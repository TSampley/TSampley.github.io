Miscommunication is something I have lots of experience with. As far back as I can remember

This has led me to the conclusion that all miscommunication is based in misaligned expectations.
Many people communicate through observational statements, with there being some understood implication that follows, necessarily based on an individual's perception of the world/value-system.
Virtue Signalling; Dog Whistles; InGroup/OutGroup
