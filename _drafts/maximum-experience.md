Let me get right into it - I believe it is our obligation to maximize the human population. I want to immediately clarify that I am not an anti-choice, pro-birther: I believe in the right to abortion and I think it is a necessary - though unfortunate - compassionate course of action when someone is in a desperate situation and sees no possible positive outcome for their potential child.*
The main idea is this: we each have unique, individual experiences, and our ability to make sense of the world around us depends on our ability to acknowledge and resolve the differences in our experiences. The individual is the quantum of experience. The more quanta we have scattered around the world gathering those unique experiences, the better we can resolve an accurate image of this existence we all share. Now, I don't believe simply having as many children as possible will lead to a productive variety of perspectives. All our experiences mean nothing in isolation - it is our ability to come together, share our experiences, and glean some knowledge from our differences that provides value to us all. In fact, it is our inability to communicate and resolve conflicts that leads to violence, reducing our population.
Secondly, we need to strive to heighten† the human experience as much as possible, we do not need to create conflict or suffering - those two are inevitable in our world; suffering is a property of existence, and conflicts arise naturally from differing views. Similarly, I don't believe that pursuing representatives of all possible ideas is necessary either. There will always be fascists: people twisted by cruelty, seeking a sense of control. There will always be bigots: people ignoring information or the experiences of others. We don't need to foster these representatives. The tolerance of intolerance supports - and is itself a form of - intolerance. We don't need to try to introduce negative experiences to improve the resolution of our shared experience, we will always have plenty to draw from; although, it's critical to hear those unfortunate enough to sample from this negative space. Their experiences reveal something about our reality many of us are fortunate enough to ignore, but by ignoring their experience we preclude incorporation of that part of reality and our society's ability to avoid perpetuating or recreating similar experiences.
An increase in population is not bound to improve the exchange of ideas, and improving the experience of a select few will not give us a better picture of reality. By improving our ability to communicate and understand one another, we will improve our lived experiences and reduce conflict, allowing us to maintain a higher population with a greater variety of experiences, increasing our understanding of our shared world, enabling us to better navigate and further improve our world.‡ Finding new ways to communicate and understand each other should therefore be our primary focus.
Asides
* - If relying on adoption or the foster system does not make someone feel secure in their child's happiness, I just consider that person rational.
† - "Heighten" in the platonic sense - through universal education, empowering every individual to reach a maximum potential.
‡ - Spreading misinformation or intentionally causing discord is understood as an existential threat.
