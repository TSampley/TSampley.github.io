Let me just start by being clear: I hate this idea. Yes, "hate" is a strong word, but I feel very strongly any time I hear someone talk about it. One of my favorite podcasts is "Philosophy for Our Times", published by the Institute of Art and Ideas, which was ironically founded by Hillary Lawson - ironic because he seems to be a leader in promoting panpsychism.
I don't understand why this ridiculous idea has gained popularity in recent years. Maybe it appeals to a sense of mysticism? I heard on the latest episode of "Science Vs." that 40% of the US believe ghosts are real. What a horrifying idea. It's not that I'm not open to the possibility, given evidence, but where's the evidence? I remember shouting at my car radio when Hillary once made the claim
