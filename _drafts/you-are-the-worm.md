At some point we became humans,
and before that we were primates like chimps, 
and before that we were mammals like rodents,
and before that we were chordates like fish,
and before that we were bilaterians like worms.

All our modern human traits derive from some point in our genetic lineage – how far back any goes is sometimes a topic of interest for anthropology nerds, and while we may not retain many original traits from our earliest heritage, we do still retain them in forms that vary in their degree of divergence.

On a very basic level, we still have a "front" and "back" where food goes in and out, respectively; it's a good body plan – one that has been retained in its basic form despite the many modifications along its entire length across many points in our evolution.
