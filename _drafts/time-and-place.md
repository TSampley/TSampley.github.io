date: 2026-07-17 1028 -0500

Every leader should be willing to explain the purpose of individual orders or a plan, but there is a time and place for everything. A live battlefield is not the time to ask why your commanding officer is asking you to run to a specific location and relay a message, nor is it the time to ask why foxholes are dug the way they are. Ideally that would have been covered during training, but some people manage to follow instructions mindlessly, only to finally find themselves curious at the worst of times.

Why do people ask so many annoying questions when you just want them to do the right thing?
People want to understand why they're being told to do something
- they like to feel included as a peer, not just a subordinate
- they want to know what they're contributing to
- they don't necessarily know that you're telling them to do "the right thing"
