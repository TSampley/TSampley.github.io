I've seen a few articles about Server-Driven UI - it seems to be the new kid on the block. I wouldn't expect it to be a new idea, but Google trends only shows regular activity starting in 2020. (side-note: the original unhyphenated form seems to be overtaking the hyphenated form)
You're just building a web browser with more steps. I know, I know - "the unique value of a native experience" - mobile web browsers are more tightly integrated with their mobile environments
So why am I, a native mobile developer, saying this? Because truth matters to me more than a job market bloated by wasted human effort.
